# Communication Preferences

- Writes and expects communication in informal Indonesian (casual, often with typos, Jakarta slang like "lu"/"oi", very short messages); responds best to Indonesian explanations. Confidence: 0.85
- Wants detailed, in-depth explanations ("analisis mendalam", "jawab detail", "jelaskan") rather than terse summaries — states it explicitly and repeatedly ("analisi emndalam"), even when re-asking a question he feels was already answered. Confidence: 0.9
- Questions shorthand/jargon the agent uses (e.g. replies just "apa itu free") and expects a concrete plain-language explanation of what it actually means in practice — including the risks/limitations — not a restatement of the label. Confidence: 0.55
- Gives only rough clues about the desired outcome and expects the agent to infer the execution details, but the *intent and design direction* must still be confirmed rather than guessed. Confidence: 0.6
- Explicitly wants you to ask when in doubt instead of assuming, and to correct precisely once intent is clarified ("ralat dan lakukan dengan presisi, jika ragu tanyakan"). Guessing wrong on intent gets a firm correction ("terjadi kesalahan maksud"). Confidence: 0.85
- Often asks for confirmation of understanding ("apa lu benar udah paham sepenuhnya?", "apa hasilnya", "udah paham?") — appreciates an explicit restatement/summary of what was understood before work continues. Confidence: 0.7
- When corrected, wants the agent to actually explain the design reasoning (why something was removed or changed), not just silently comply. Confidence: 0.65
